# Toronto-Metropolitan-Aerial-Vehicles-2024-TMAV-

TMAV is a student run extracurricular activity/club that designs and develops autonomous aerial vehicles within the constrains of a end of year competition.
The 2024 competition took place in Marylands 22nd annual Sudent Unmanned Aerial Systems (SUAS) Competition.T
The constrains that we had to work with were besides take off and landing, the aerial vehicle had to be completely autonomous.
The vehicle had for the competition had to have obstacle detection, obstacle avoidance, and air drop.
This meant that our vehicle had to autonomously detect the parameter of the obstacle, find its intended targets/air drop locations, and safely deploy and land the given load/package.
